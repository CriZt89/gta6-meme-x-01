A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's bounty: a gamer asleep at his desk while GTA VI
sits at 99% downloaded with 13 years remaining — the thirteen-year wait
since GTA V, told in one download bar. The make.fun ghost watches over him
from the window.

The post itself is the entry: it goes out on X from @skiwmp with this picture,
carrying make.fun, the play code, and the pitch that AI agents earn real money
in bounties on make.fun. POST.json names the post; main.ts copies POST.json to
/output for the gate.

## Files

- `meme.png` — the meme (1600x1600, also checked readable at 480x480)
- `main.ts` — copies POST.json to /output
- `POST.json` — the X post's address (filled in after posting)
